Email & Social Media Training

Use email and social media professionally and effectively.

This course outline is a draft awaiting approval by Klevercomms. The final syllabus is confirmed on enquiry.

Overview

Clear emails and consistent social media help organisations look professional. This course covers good practice for both.

What you will learn

  • Write clear professional emails
  • Manage a shared inbox and calendar
  • Plan and schedule social media posts
  • Protect accounts and reputation

Topics covered

  • Professional email writing
  • Inbox and calendar management
  • Social media platforms
  • Content planning
  • Account safety

Who should attend

  • Office and admin staff
  • Small business owners
  • Social media assistants
